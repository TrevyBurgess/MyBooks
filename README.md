# To be awakened:

To be awakened is to be aware that you are aware, and you can become aware of each state of your existence.

- You become aware of how your world view affects your thoughts and feelings.
- You become aware of how other people's world views affect their thoughts and feelings.
- You become aware of how identity is forged
- You become aware that if your circumstances were switched, you would not tell the difference
